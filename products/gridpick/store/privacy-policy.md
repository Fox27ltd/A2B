# Gridpick — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 7 September 2026

## The short version

Gridpick does not collect, store, transmit or sell any of your data. Everything happens inside your own browser.

## What Gridpick reads

When you click the Gridpick icon on a page, it reads that page's visible content in order to find tables and lists. That content is processed entirely on your own device, in your own browser, and is used only to build the file you asked for.

Gridpick does not read any page until you click it, and only reads the tab you clicked it on.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, with one exception: if you buy Gridpick Pro, the payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe, who receive your email address and payment details in order to process the transaction and tell Gridpick whether your licence is active. Gridpick never sees your card details. See ExtensionPay's and Stripe's own privacy policies for how they handle that data.

Files you export are saved to your own computer by your browser. They are not uploaded anywhere.

## What Gridpick stores

Locally, in your browser's extension storage, and nowhere else:

- Your preferred export format
- Whether your Pro licence is currently active

You can erase all of it by removing the extension.

## Analytics and tracking

There are none. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no cookies.

## Permissions, and why each exists

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| activeTab | Read the page you clicked Gridpick on, to find its tables |
| scripting | Run the extraction code in that tab |
| storage | Remember your export format and licence status, locally |
| downloads | Save the file you asked for |
| contextMenus | Add the right-click menu entry |
| tabs, host access | Requested only if you tick "Follow next page links", so the following pages can be read. Never requested otherwise. |

## Children

Gridpick is a tool for working with data and is not directed at children under 13.
